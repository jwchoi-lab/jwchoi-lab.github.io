Jongwoo Choi

Jongwoo Choi

I am a postdoctoral fellow in the Department of Biostatistics at Harvard University, working with Prof. Jeffrey W. Miller. Prior to joining Harvard, I received my Ph.D. in Statistics from the University of Connecticut, where I was advised by Prof. Neil A. Spencer and Prof. Dipak K. Dey.

My current primary research areas are

  • Robustness to model misspecification,
  • Intersection of Bayesian statistics and AI,
  • Applications in psychiatry and cognitive neuroscience.
